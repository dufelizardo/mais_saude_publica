# Professionals

> **Version:** 1.15.0 · [Português](../pt-BR/19-profissionais.md)

Menu: **Recursos Humanos › Profissionais** (Human resources › Professionals). It is the network's staff list and each person's
profile, with their working life:

- posting and pay;
- leave and timekeeping;
- occupational health and safety;
- training, appraisals and benefits;
- termination.

**Who uses it:**

| Profile | What they do |
|---------|--------------|
| Anyone signed in | views the staff list: name, role, unit and council |
| HR management (manager) | registers, terminates and maintains profiles |

> **What changes over time becomes history.** Posting, job and salary are not edited: each change is a new dated record, and the
> previous one stays in the employment history.

## 1. The staff list

**The cards at the top:**

- **Profissionais ativos** (active professionals);
- **Médicos** (physicians) and **Enfermagem** (nursing);
- **Em férias / afastados** (on holiday / on leave).

**Searching and filtering:**

1. Search by name, CPF, council registration or staff number.
2. Filter by **Unidade**, **Função** (role), **Especialidade** (specialty) and **Situação** (status): active, without posting,
   terminated or all.
3. View as cards or as a list: the professional, category, role and unit, council and working hours.

On each professional:

- **Ver perfil** (view profile);
- **Ver escala** (view roster);
- **Atribuir equipe** (assign team);
- **Desligar** (terminate).

## 2. Registering a professional

1. Click to register (or **Cadastrar o primeiro** — register the first one — when the list is empty).
2. **Identificação** (identification):
   - the **CPF** and the **Nome completo** (full name);
   - the **Conselho de classe** (professional council: CRM, COREN, CRO…) and the **Número do conselho** (council number),
     optional. The council sets the professional's category on screen (Physicians, Nursing…).
3. **Contato e admissão** (contact and hiring):
   - the **E-mail**, **Telefone** (phone) and **Data de admissão** (hiring date);
   - the **Lotação de admissão** (initial posting): the **Unidade de saúde** (health unit) and the **Cargo** (job).
4. **Endereço** (address): the **CEP** (postcode) and the other fields.
5. Save. **The staff number is generated automatically.** The initial posting is recorded with it; the job and unit can change
   later, through the profile.

## 3. Terminating

1. On the professional, click **Desligar** and enter the **Data de desligamento** (termination date).
2. Confirm (*Profissional desligado*).

The record and employment history remain. Severance and other settlements are on the profile's **Desligamento/Rescisão**
(termination/severance) tab.

## 4. The professional's profile

The header shows the category, status, current posting and working hours. The profile has 13 tabs:

| Tab | What it has | Action |
|-----|-------------|--------|
| **Dados** (details) | contact, address and employment relationship | **Editar contato** (edit contact) |
| **Lotação** (posting) | the current posting (unit, sector, job and hours) and its history | **+ Registrar transferência** (record transfer) |
| **Composição remuneratória** (pay composition) | the salary calculated on the fly from the posting, the salary table, the length-of-service bonus and current adjustments; it is not the payroll | — |
| **Ajustes individuais** (individual adjustments) | personal bonus, court-ordered equalisation and others | **+ Registrar ajuste** |
| **Afastamentos e Licenças** (leave) | holiday, sick leave, personal leave; maternity, paternity, illness, work accident, bereavement, marriage | **+ Registrar afastamento** / **+ Registrar licença** |
| **Ponto** (timekeeping) | clock-ins: start, break start and end, finish | **+ Registrar ponto** |
| **Folha de pagamento** (payroll) | the professional's payslips | **+ Registrar folha** |
| **SST** (occupational health and safety) | occupational exams, work accidents and PPE | **+ Registrar exame** / **acidente** / **EPI** |
| **Treinamentos** (training) | participations and **Competências desenvolvidas** (competencies developed) | **+ Registrar participação** |
| **Avaliações** (appraisals) | performance appraisals | **+ Registrar avaliação** |
| **Benefícios** (benefits) | current and closed enrolments | **+ Nova adesão** (new enrolment) |
| **Desligamento/Rescisão** | the severance calculation | **+ Registrar cálculo de rescisão** |
| **Histórico funcional** (employment history) | the timeline: transfers, postings, leave, adjustments and termination | — |

## 5. Transferring, changing sector or job

On the **Lotação** tab, click **+ Registrar transferência**.

1. Choose the **Unidade** and, optionally, the unit's **Setor** (sector, active). With a sector, payroll goes to the sector's cost
   centre.
2. Choose the **Cargo** and enter the weekly hours, the start date and the reason.
3. Save (*Transferência registrada*).

The current posting is closed, and the new one takes effect. Changing unit, sector or job is always a new posting.

## 6. Timekeeping and corrections

1. Use **+ Registrar ponto** for a clock-in: start, break start, break end or finish.
2. To correct a clock-in, request a **correção** (correction): the proposed type and date and time, with the reason. The entry
   shows **Correção pendente** (correction pending; *Aguarda aprovação* — awaiting approval).
3. Management approves (*O registro foi atualizado* — the record was updated) or rejects (*O registro continua como estava* — the
   record stays as it was).

## 7. Occupational health and safety (SST)

- **Exames ocupacionais** (occupational exams):
  - the type: pre-employment, periodic, exit, return to work or change of role;
  - the result: fit, unfit or fit with restrictions.
- **Acidentes de trabalho** (work accidents): the staff member's accident record.
- **EPIs** (PPE): the equipment issued, **Em uso** (in use) or **Devolvido** (returned).

## 8. Severance

On the **Desligamento/Rescisão** tab, **+ Registrar cálculo de rescisão** stores the calculation, with the reason:

- without cause or with cause;
- resignation or end of contract;
- retirement or transfer.

## 9. Common messages

| Message | What it means | What to do |
|---------|---------------|------------|
| *Não foi possível registrar a transferência. Tente novamente.* — the transfer could not be recorded | the transfer was not saved | check the unit, the sector (same unit and active) and the job |
| *Não foi possível registrar o ajuste. Tente novamente.* — the adjustment could not be recorded | the adjustment was not saved | check the type, amount and dates |
| *Tipo é obrigatório.* / *Data/hora é obrigatória.* — type / date-time is required | the timekeeping correction is incomplete | fill in the type and date and time |
| *Não foi possível atualizar o contato. Tente novamente.* — the contact could not be updated | the contact was not saved | check the e-mail and phone |
