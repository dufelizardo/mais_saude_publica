# Coordination and management

> **Version:** 1.15.0 · [Português](../pt-BR/29-coordenacoes-e-gerencia.md)

Menu: **Administrativo › Coordenações e gerência** (coordination and management). It is the health department's management
structure:

- who is responsible for each area;
- each region's indicators.

**Who uses it:** administrative management (manager) maintains it. Those who read administration or view indicators can see it.

## 1. The organisation chart

The structure is **Department → management offices → coordinations → technical supervisions**.

**The cards at the top:**

- **Gerências** (management offices, by theme);
- **Coordenações** (coordinations);
- **Supervisões técnicas** (technical supervisions: programmes, quality, surveillance);
- areas **Com titular** (with a holder).

**Coordenações sem gerência** (coordinations without a management office) appear separately, to be fixed.

**Creating an area:**

1. Click **Nova área** (new area) and choose the **Tipo** (type: management office, coordination or technical supervision) and
   the **Tema** (theme).
2. Enter:
   - the **Nome** (name, for example *Coordenação Regional Centro* — Central Regional Coordination) and the **Sigla**
     (acronym);
   - the parent area and the **Regional** (region; optional, for a regional coordination);
   - the **Descrição**.
3. Leave **Ativa** (active) ticked and save.

## 2. Who is responsible

Choose the area in the chart.

1. Use **Designar titular** (appoint holder) or **Designar substituto** (appoint deputy).
2. Choose the **Profissional** and enter the **Início** (start) and the **Ato de designação** (appointment act, for example
   *Portaria 45/2026* — Ordinance 45/2026).
3. Save. **The current holder's term ends the day before the new one starts**, and the history is kept.

**Histórico de quem respondeu** (history of who was responsible) shows them all. **Encerrar hoje** (end today) ends the
appointment.

## 3. Regional indicators

For a regional coordination, **Indicadores da regional · mês corrente** (regional indicators, current month) show:

- encounters and doses given;
- programme targets and reports.

The figures add up the units below the region, as in the Situation Room when choosing the region. The list shows the units and
which are **Em operação** (in operation).

## 4. Common messages

| Situation | What it means | What to do |
|-----------|---------------|------------|
| *Nenhuma área de gestão cadastrada* — no management area registered | the chart is empty | create the areas |
| **Coordenações sem gerência** | the coordination has no parent area | edit it and choose the management office |
| *Ninguém designado ainda.* — nobody appointed yet | the area has no holder | appoint the holder |
| *Sem acesso aos indicadores desta regional.* — no access to this region's indicators | your profile does not view the region's indicators | ask for the profile, if your role needs it |
