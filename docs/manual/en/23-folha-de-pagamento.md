# Payroll

> **Version:** 1.15.0 · [Português](../pt-BR/23-folha-de-pagamento.md)

Menu: **Recursos Humanos › Folha de pagamento** (Human resources › Payroll). It is the payroll view by accounting month
(*competência*). **Each professional's payroll is recorded on the profile's Folha de pagamento tab** (module 19).

**Who uses it:** HR management (manager).

## 1. Viewing the month

1. Choose the accounting month.
2. The cards show:
   - **Folhas processadas** (payslips processed);
   - **Proventos** (earnings: salary and allowances);
   - **Descontos + encargos** (deductions and employer charges);
   - **Total da competência** (month total);
   - **Profissionais processados** (professionals processed).
3. The list shows each professional, with earnings, deductions, charges and total. Search by name or staff number.

## 2. Recording a professional's payroll

1. Use **Ir para Profissionais** (go to Professionals) and open the profile.
2. On the **Folha de pagamento** tab, use **+ Registrar folha** (record payroll) for the month.

> The profile's **Composição remuneratória** (pay composition) is calculated on the fly from the posting, the salary table, the
> length-of-service bonus and current adjustments. It helps check the payroll, but it is not the payroll.

## 3. Payroll and costs

Recorded payroll goes into Finance's cost calculation. It goes to the cost centre of the posting's sector or,
without a sector, to the unit's general centre.

## 4. Common messages

| Situation | What it means | What to do |
|-----------|---------------|------------|
| *Registre a folha de cada profissional na aba Folha de pagamento do perfil.* — record each professional's payroll on the profile | the month has no payroll yet | record it through the profile |
| *Nenhum profissional com essa busca* — no professional matches the search | the name or number is not in the month | check the search |
