# Health programmes

> **Version:** 1.15.0 · [Português](../pt-BR/09-programas-de-saude.md)

Menu: **Assistência › Programas de Saúde** (Care › Health programmes). It brings together the programmes the network offers (for
example, hypertension and diabetes, antenatal care or child health): who takes part, programmed actions, campaigns, care
pathways and indicators.

**Who uses it:**

| Profile | What they do |
|---------|--------------|
| Manager | registers programmes, categories, participating units, care pathways, campaigns and targets |
| Those who follow up: physicians, nursing, oral health, psychology and social work | enrol patients, mark defaulters, record exits, plan and record actions |
| Anyone signed in | views the programme catalogue |

**The cards at the top:**

- the **Programas ativos** (active programmes) offered in the network;
- the **Acompanhamentos vigentes** (current follow-ups): patients in programmes;
- the **Faltosos / atrasados** (defaulters / overdue), who need active search.

The screen has five tabs:

- **Programas** (programmes);
- **Ações programáticas** (programmed actions);
- **Campanhas** (campaigns);
- **Linhas de cuidado** (care pathways);
- **A convocar (saúde bucal)** (to call in, oral health).

## 1. The Programas tab

1. Search by name or theme and filter by category and unit.
2. Click the programme to see:
   - the **Público-alvo** (target population): who, the conditions (ICD-10 or ICPC-2) and the period;
   - the **Unidades e equipes participantes** (participating units and teams);
   - the figures: enrolled patients, defaulters and overdue (no visit in the period).

> The target population describes whom the programme serves. **Enrolment is always done by a professional.**

A **Sensível** (sensitive) programme is one where taking part reveals a diagnosis (mental health, STI). Only those who follow up
at the unit can see that the patient takes part.

## 2. Enrolling a patient

**Who uses it:** those who follow up.

1. In the programme, click **Inscrever paciente** (enrol patient).
2. Under **Paciente**, search by name, CPF or SUS card.
3. Choose the **Unidade responsável** (responsible unit; only those offering the programme) and the **Equipe** (team), or **Sem
   equipe** (no team).
4. Optionally, the **Profissional responsável (matrícula)** (responsible professional's staff number). Save.

**Eligible:** the **Elegíveis** list shows enrolled residents of the territory not yet in the programme.

1. Choose the team and micro-area. The list shows the territory's patients who fit the programme's target population.
2. Use **Inscrever** (enrol) on each one.

Without a team with an area in the Territory, the list is empty: it comes from the families' enrolment.

## 3. Following up patients

Under **Ver pacientes** (view patients), the list of those followed up at the units you have access to shows:

- since when the patient has been in the programme and the last visit;
- the status, with the **Atrasado** (overdue) mark when follow-up is late.

Filter by **Todos** (all), defaulters or **Atrasados** (overdue). The actions are:

| Action | What it asks for |
|--------|------------------|
| **Marcar faltoso** (mark defaulter) / **Voltar a ativo** (back to active) | the reason |
| **Busca ativa** (active search) | creates the active search action for the defaulter or overdue patient; the suggested person responsible is the micro-area's community health worker |
| **Saída** (exit) | the **Motivo da saída** (exit reason: discharge with targets met, transfer, move to another municipality, dropout, death or other), the description and the date |

**Discharge completes the follow-up; the other reasons close it.** The record stays in the patient's history.

## 4. Programmed actions

**What it is for:** what is planned for each patient or group, such as a scheduled consultation, a measurement, a test, a
vaccine, a home visit, an active search, a group, guidance or a mobilisation.

1. Filter by programme, period (this week, 4 weeks or this month) and status.
2. To create one, click **Prever ação** (plan action):
   - choose the **Programa** and the type of action;
   - **Para** (for): **Um paciente acompanhado** (a followed-up patient) or **Um grupo (atividade coletiva)** (a group, collective
     activity), with the participants;
   - enter the **Unidade**, the **Local** (place), the person responsible and the **Data prevista** (planned date).
3. On each action in the list:
   - **Agendar** (book) opens the **Agenda** (module 02) to book the action;
   - **Confirmar** (confirm) records that it was done;
   - **Não realizada** (not done) and **Cancelar** (cancel) ask for the reason.

A **Vencida** (overdue) action has passed its planned date without a record. **Pronta para confirmar** (ready to confirm) is an
action whose booking already had its encounter completed: confirm it with the suggested encounter. **Educação em saúde** (health
education) and **Saúde bucal** (oral health) actions open their own record.

> A booking made in the Schedule with the programme (module 02) fulfils the patient's scheduled consultation.

## 5. Campaigns

1. Click **Nova campanha** (new campaign; manager).
2. Enter:
   - the **Nome** (name, for example *Vacinação contra a gripe 2026* — flu vaccination 2026);
   - the **Tipo** (type): vaccination, screening or prevention, awareness, joint effort or other;
   - the **Programa**, if any, the **Início** (start) and **Fim** (end);
   - the **Descrição**, which appears in the public listing.
3. Define the population: minimum and maximum age, sex and, optionally, **Público em palavras** (population in words, for
   example *crianças de 6 meses a 5 anos e gestantes* — children aged 6 months to 5 years and pregnant women).
4. Enter the **Meta** (target, for example *90% de cobertura* — 90% coverage) and the number of people. For vaccination
   campaigns, choose the **Vacinas da campanha** (campaign vaccines).
5. Save. The campaign goes through planned, ongoing, closed or cancelled, and can be **Publicada** (published).

## 6. Care pathways

**What it is for:** the path the patient takes in care, in ordered steps. For example, antenatal care from the last menstrual
period, or a child from birth.

**The catalogue (manager):**

1. Click **Nova linha** (new pathway) and enter the **Código** (code), the **Nome** and the **Programa** (optional).
2. Build the **Etapas, na ordem** (steps, in order).
3. Leave **Ativa** (active) ticked.

**Pathways in progress:**

1. Under **Percursos em curso** (pathways in progress), filter by pathway or **Só atrasados** (overdue only).
2. To open a pathway, use **Abrir a linha** (open the pathway) in **Sugestões** (suggestions: programme patients not yet on the
   pathway), or from the patient (module 01, Programmes tab). Enter the **Unidade** and the **Início** (start: last menstrual
   period, birth or suspicion).
3. In the pathway, each step has **Registrar** (record: done) or **Dispensar** (waive: not applicable).
4. At the end, **Concluir o percurso** (complete the pathway, with an optional note). To stop earlier, use **Interromper**
   (interrupt), with the reason.

## 7. To call in (oral health)

The tab lists people who need dental treatment and do not yet have an open plan. They are the people seen in the collective
assessment (at school, nursery or in the community).

1. Choose the unit.
2. Use **Abrir prontuário** (open record) to call the person in and start treatment.

## 8. Indicators and targets

Under **Ver indicadores** (view indicators), the programme shows each indicator's targets, clinical control and progress.

- Choose the period (**Até** — until) and **Onde** (where: the unit or **Toda a rede** — the whole network).
- Each indicator shows the value and the progress chart.
- **Exportar CSV** downloads the figures.

**Targets (manager):**

1. Click **Nova meta** (new target).
2. Choose the **Indicador**, enter the **Alvo** (target value), the **Início** and the **Fim**.
3. For a test indicator, enter the **Exame** (test) and the minimum and maximum control limits.
4. Save. **Editar** (edit) and **Excluir** (delete) adjust the target.

> The indicators of a sensitive programme are only for those who follow up its patients.

## 9. Registering and maintaining programmes (manager)

**The programme:**

1. Click to create a programme and enter:
   - the **Código** and the **Categoria**;
   - the **Nome** and the **Descrição**;
   - the **Abrangência** (scope: national, state or municipal), the **Início** and the **Fim**;
   - the **Periodicidade do acompanhamento** (follow-up frequency), from monthly to yearly, or none;
   - the **Coordenação (matrícula)** (coordinator's staff number).
2. Define the target population:
   - the minimum and maximum age;
   - the sex (any, female, male or pregnant women);
   - the **Condições (CID-10 ou CIAP-2)** (conditions), separated by commas (for example, *I10, E11, T90*).
3. Tick **Programa sensível** when taking part reveals a diagnosis.

**Participating units:**

- **Incluir unidade** (add unit): the **Unidade** and the **Equipe**, or **A unidade toda** (the whole unit). Only the chosen
  unit's active teams appear.
- **Encerrar** (end): the participation, with the reason (for example, *a equipe passou a outra UBS* — the team moved to another
  health centre).

**Categories:** **Nova categoria** (new category), with its order. An inactive category gets no new programmes.

The **Exportar relatório** (export report) button at the top downloads the list of programmes.

## 10. Common messages

| Message | What it means | What to do |
|---------|---------------|------------|
| *Nenhuma unidade oferece este programa ainda.* — no unit offers this programme yet | the programme has no participating unit | the manager adds the unit |
| *Escolha a equipe para ver os adscritos que cabem no público do programa.* — choose the team to see the eligible residents | the eligible list depends on the team | choose the team |
| *Nenhum elegível fora do programa.* — no eligible person outside the programme | everyone in the target population is already in | nothing to do |
| *Os indicadores deste programa sensível são de quem acompanha os pacientes.* — this sensitive programme's indicators are for those who follow up | your profile does not follow up the programme | ask for the profile, if your role needs it |
| *Escolha o indicador e informe o alvo.* — choose the indicator and enter the target | the target is incomplete | choose the indicator and the value |
| *Período inválido.* — invalid period | the start is after the end | correct the dates |
| *Seu acesso não inclui este percurso.* — your access does not include this pathway | the pathway is from another unit or is sensitive | ask for the profile, if your role needs it |
