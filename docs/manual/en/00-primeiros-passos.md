# Getting started

> **Version:** 1.15.0 · [Português](../pt-BR/00-primeiros-passos.md)

What every user needs to know before using any screen: signing in, looking after the password, finding the right screen in the
menu and understanding the notices. The application is in Portuguese; labels are quoted as they appear on screen, with a
translation.

## 1. Signing in

**What it is for:** opening your work session.

**Who uses it:** every professional or manager with an account. If you do not have one yet, ask your unit's coordination.

**Step by step:**

1. Open the system address given by the Health Department and click to sign in.
2. Choose how to identify yourself: the **CPF** tab (Brazilian taxpayer number) or the **Matrícula** tab (staff number).
3. Type the CPF (digits only; the dots and the dash are added automatically) or your staff number.
4. Type the password (**Senha**). The eye icon shows or hides what you typed.
5. If the computer or phone is yours alone, tick **Manter conectado neste dispositivo** (keep me signed in on this device).
6. Click **Entrar** (sign in).

**How long the session lasts:**

| Situation | The session ends |
|-----------|------------------|
| Without **Manter conectado** | when the browser is closed, or after 8 hours |
| With **Manter conectado** | after 7 days, or when you click **Sair** (sign out) |

When the session ends, the system goes back to the sign-in screen by itself. Sign in again.

> Do not tick **Manter conectado** on a shared computer, such as the reception desk or the vaccination room.

**Accessibility:** at the top of the sign-in screen, the **A**, **A+** and **A++** buttons change the font size, and **Contraste**
turns on high contrast.

## 2. First access and temporary password

When the administration creates your account or resets your password, it gives you a **temporary password**. On the first access
with it, the system opens the **Crie a sua senha** (create your password) screen before any other.

1. In **Senha provisória** (temporary password), type the password you received.
2. In **Nova senha** (new password) and **Confirmar nova senha** (confirm), type a password only you will know.
3. Check the list of requirements below. Each item gets a ✓ when it is met:
   - from 8 to 72 characters;
   - different from the current password;
   - not your CPF;
   - the confirmation matches the new password.
4. Click **Salvar nova senha** (save new password).

Until the temporary password is changed, the system does not open any other screen. The **Sair** button on this screen signs you
out without changing it.

## 3. Changing the password

1. In the top right corner, click the key icon (**Trocar senha**, change password).
2. Type the **Senha atual** (current password), the **Nova senha** and the confirmation, with the same requirements as in item 2.
3. Click **Salvar nova senha**. The new password applies from the next access, on any device.

## 4. Forgot the password or got locked out

- **Esqueci minha senha** (forgot my password): if the link is active on the sign-in screen, enter your CPF. The system sends a
  link to the e-mail in your professional record so you can create a new password. If it does not arrive, check the spam folder;
  you can ask again in a few minutes. If the link is greyed out, the system administration resets the password: ask for a
  temporary password and follow item 2.
- **Lockout after failed attempts:** after **5 wrong passwords in a row**, the account is locked for **15 minutes**. Wait and try
  again, or ask the administration to unlock it sooner.

## 5. The main screen

After signing in, the screen has three parts:

- **The menu, on the left:** the screens your profile allows. The groups (**Recursos Humanos** — human resources,
  **Administrativo** — administration of the network, **Assistência** — care, **Vigilância** — surveillance, and
  **Administração** — system administration) open and close with a click. On a phone, the menu is hidden: use the **Abrir menu**
  (open menu) button at the top.
- **The top bar:** the trail (for example, *Início › Assistência › Agenda*), which shows where you are; your name and staff
  number; the key to change the password; and the **Sair** button.
- **The content:** the chosen screen.

Some items appear at the top of the menu only when there is something of yours to see, with a number next to them:

| Item | Appears when |
|------|--------------|
| **Meus contratos** (my contracts) | there are contracts you follow up, with the number of orders awaiting delivery |
| **Meus bens** (my assets) | there are assets under your responsibility |
| **Meus inventários** (my inventories) | you are on a committee with an open inventory |

## 6. Profile and unit: why I see (or do not see) a screen

- What appears in the menu and the buttons on each screen depend on the **profile** the administration granted you, according to
  your role (for example, reception, nursing, pharmacy or management).
- Each profile applies to a **unit** or to a set of units (for example, all the units of a region). You see and record data for
  the units where you have the profile.
- **If a screen or button you need is missing,** ask the coordination or the administration for the right profile. Do not use
  someone else's password.
- **If a button appears for a moment and disappears** when the screen opens, your profile does not allow that action.

## 7. How screens work

Internal screens follow the same pattern:

- **Tabs:** separate the topics of the same screen (for example, *Estoque* — stock, *Dispensações* — dispensing, and
  *Medicamentos* — medicines, in the Pharmacy).
- **Cards at the top:** the current figures, such as pending, overdue and this month.
- **Lists and tables:** the records, with search and filters above them.
- **Drawer:** creating, editing and recording open a panel on the right, without leaving the screen.
  - Required fields warn below, in red, when they are missing or wrong.
  - The confirm button is at the end of the drawer.
  - The **X**, the **Esc** key or a click outside the drawer close it without saving.
- **Confirmation notice:** after saving, a short message confirms what was done (for example, *Transferência registrada* —
  transfer recorded).
- **Clinical records are not deleted:** triage, progress notes, consultations and procedures are corrected by **retificação**
  (amendment), with a reason. The original record is kept.

## 8. Common messages

| Message | What it means | What to do |
|---------|---------------|------------|
| *Informe um CPF válido (11 dígitos).* — enter a valid CPF (11 digits) | the CPF is incomplete | check the digits |
| *A senha deve ter ao menos 8 caracteres.* — the password must have at least 8 characters | the password typed is too short | type the full password |
| *CPF/matrícula ou senha inválidos.* — invalid CPF/staff number or password | the CPF, staff number or password do not match | check and try again; after 5 errors the account locks |
| *Conta temporariamente bloqueada por excesso de tentativas.* — account temporarily locked after too many attempts | there were 5 wrong passwords in a row | wait 15 minutes or ask the administration to unlock it |
| *A senha atual não confere.* — the current password does not match | when changing the password, the current one is wrong | type the password you use today |
| *A nova senha não pode ser o CPF.* — the new password cannot be the CPF | the new password is your CPF | choose another password |
| Went back to the sign-in screen by itself | the session ended | sign in again |

## 9. Privacy and care with data

- The system stores health data, protected by Brazil's data protection law (LGPD).
- **The audit trail records** who viewed the details of a patient or a professional, and every change: who did it, when and in
  which unit.
- **The password is personal.** Whatever is done with it is recorded under your name.
- **When you leave the computer,** click **Sair**, especially on a shared machine.
