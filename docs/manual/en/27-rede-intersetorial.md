# Intersectoral network

> **Version:** 1.15.0 · [Português](../pt-BR/27-rede-intersetorial.md)

Menu: **Administrativo › Rede intersetorial** (intersectoral network). It is the catalogue of institutions outside health to
which cases are referred. For example:

- social assistance (CRAS and CREAS social assistance centres, Centro POP for homeless people);
- education;
- rights protection (Guardianship Council, councils, Public Defender's Office, Public Prosecutor's Office, courts);
- work and income, housing and public security.

The catalogue is used by Mental health (referrals), by Surveillance (mandatory communication of violence) and by those who refer.

**Who uses it:**

| Profile | What they do |
|---------|--------------|
| Anyone signed in | views the catalogue |
| Managers and social workers | maintain the catalogue and follow the dashboard |

## 1. The catalogue

Search by name or contact person, filter by **Setor** (sector) and choose **Ativas** (active) or **Com as inativas** (including
inactive).

**Registering an institution:**

1. Click **Nova instituição** (new institution).
2. Enter the **Nome** (for example, *CRAS Centro*), the **Setor** and the **Tipo** (type).
3. Optionally, the **Endereço** (address), **Telefone** (phone), **E-mail**, **Pessoa de contato** (contact person) and an
   **Observação** (note, for example *atende os bairros do centro* — serves the central districts).
4. Leave **Ativa no catálogo** (active in the catalogue) ticked. An inactive one receives no new referrals; existing ones continue.

## 2. The dashboard

The **Painel** (dashboard) tab shows referrals by institution.

1. Filter by **Enviados desde** (sent from) and **Enviados até** (sent until), by **Origem** (origin) and by **Unidade**.
2. The cards show:
   - **Enviados** (sent);
   - **Em aberto** (open: today, from any date);
   - **Sem retorno** (no reply: open past the deadline);
   - **Taxa de resolução** (resolution rate).
3. By institution: completed, refused, resolution and average time.

**Cobrar** (chase up): the list of referrals without a reply shows the **Número** (number), the sending date, the deadline and the
delay. To chase the institution, use the number and sending date. **The patient and the reason stay with whoever referred.**

> The Situation Room warns when there are referrals without a reply and opens this tab directly.

## 3. Common messages

| Situation | What it means | What to do |
|-----------|---------------|------------|
| *Nenhum encaminhamento sem retorno.* — no referral without a reply | all replied on time | nothing to do |
| Institution **Inativa** (inactive) | it receives no new referrals | reactivate it, if it is serving again |
