# Reports

> **Version:** 1.15.0 · [Português](../pt-BR/17-relatorios.md)

Menu: **Relatórios** (Reports, at the top of the menu). It brings together ready-made management reports, to print or export. As
in the Situation Room, they are **aggregates only, no patients**.

**Who uses it:** those who view indicators: managers, nursing coordination and the Patient Safety Office.

## 1. Generating a report

1. Under **Unidade**, choose a unit or **Todas do meu acesso** (all I can access), and under **Até** (until), the end of the
   period.
2. Use **Buscar relatório** (search report) to find it by name, or choose from the list.
3. The report opens with the indicators at the top, the sections as tables and the notes.
4. **Imprimir** (print) opens the print version. **Exportar CSV** downloads the figures. The export is recorded in the audit
   trail.

## 2. The reports

| Report | Category | What it contains |
|--------|----------|------------------|
| Produção assistencial (care production) | Care | encounters, schedule, no-shows and fit-ins in the period, with breakdowns |
| Situação operacional (operational situation) | Operations | waiting time in the period, schedule occupancy and roster issues for the coming days |
| Indicadores dos programas (programme indicators) | Programmes | enrolled, new, exits, defaulters, up to date and targets for each programme |
| Cobertura vacinal (vaccination coverage) | Epidemiology | doses given, coverage of reference vaccines and campaigns |
| Vigilância em saúde (health surveillance) | Epidemiology | reports by condition and by week, timeliness of closure and regularity |
| Campanhas de comunicação (communication campaigns) | Communication and education | reach, educational actions, territory coverage, targets and evaluation of each campaign |
| Educação em saúde (health education) | Communication and education | educational actions by theme and unit, people reached, participation and materials |
| Pactuação e cobertura da rede (network agreements and coverage) | Network management | agreed supply, quota use, over-quota, outside the reference, queue, quota per thousand enrolled, alerts and network gaps |
| Vigilância sanitária (health inspection) | Surveillance | inspections by reason and result, licences, complaints, notices by severity, processes by phase, penalties and closures |
| Qualidade do serviço (service quality) | Quality | quality indicators against targets, safety incidents, non-conformities and protocol adherence |

## 3. Common messages

| Message | What it means | What to do |
|---------|---------------|------------|
| *Nenhum relatório com essa busca.* — no report matches this search | the name searched does not exist | search for another word |
| *Sem dados no período.* — no data in the period | there are no records in the scope | change the unit or the period |
| *Não foi possível gerar o relatório.* / *Não foi possível exportar o relatório.* — the report could not be generated / exported | the server did not respond | try again |
