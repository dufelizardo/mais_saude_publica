# Communication

> **Version:** 1.15.0 · [Português](../pt-BR/28-comunicacao.md)

Menu: **Administrativo › Comunicação** (Communication). It brings together:

- **comunicados** (notices): messages to the public and to professionals;
- **conteúdos educativos** (educational content): health guidance, with technical review;
- **materials**, **channels** and **communication campaigns**.

**Who uses it:** those who publish, write or review (managers and the platform administrator).

## 1. Notices

**Creating:**

1. Enter the **Título** (title) and the **Mensagem** (message).

   > **Do not mention any person or health condition:** the notice can be read by anyone.

2. Choose the **Prioridade** (priority). Emergency priority only goes out with the emergency coordination.
3. Enter the **Início** (start) and the **Fim** (end).
4. Choose the **Unidade** (without it, the whole network) and, optionally, the **Campanha de comunicação** (communication
   campaign). The notice's period must fit within the campaign's.
5. Save.

**The cycle:**

- **Publicar** (publish) puts the notice **No ar** (live). **A published notice cannot be edited.**
- Outside its period, it leaves the portal by itself.
- **Encerrar** (end) removes it earlier.
- The **Histórico** (history) keeps the steps.

## 2. Educational content

**The cycle:** draft → technical review → approved → published. **The author cannot approve their own content.**

1. Create the content:
   - the **Título**, **Resumo** (summary) and **Texto** (text), in plain language and checked against current technical guidance;
   - the **Tema** (theme), **Faixa etária** (age group) and **Validade** (validity).
2. Use **Enviar à revisão** (send for review).
3. The reviewer uses **Aprovar** (approve) or **Devolver** (return, with the reason).
4. Once approved, publish it.
5. To change published content, use **Nova versão** (new version). Wherever the content is used, the current version is shown.

**Materials:** each content item can have materials (name, format and the link where it is already published). Use **Adicionar
material** (add material) or **Retirar** (remove).

Reviewed content appears as teaching material in trainings (module 22) and in campaigns.

## 3. Channels

The channels through which communication goes out. Only **Portal** and **E-mail** actually send; the others are recorded as not
sent. Each channel has a name, type, description and active flag.

## 4. Communication campaigns

These are different from the operational campaigns in Health programmes.

**Creating:**

1. Click **Nova campanha** (new campaign).
2. Enter:
   - the name (for example, *Outubro Rosa* — Pink October) and the **Objetivo** (objective, for example *estimular a mamografia
     entre 50 e 69 anos* — encourage mammography between 50 and 69);
   - the **Público-alvo** (target audience, for example *50 a 69 anos*) and the themes, separated by commas (for example, *câncer
     de mama, mamografia*);
   - the programme (optional) and the targets for people and actions;
   - the **Canais planejados** (planned channels), the **Conteúdos** (reviewed content only) and the **Territórios**.
3. Use **Iniciar** (start) when the campaign begins.

**Following up:** the campaign shows:

- its content, notices (created on the Notices tab, choosing the campaign) and educational actions (linked in Programmes, on the
  collective action);
- **Alcance por canal** (reach by channel): publications, sent, failures and delivery;
- **Território** (territory): micro-areas, those with an action, coverage, residents and people, and the one with **menor
  cobertura** (lowest coverage).

**Summary and evaluation:**

1. The **Balanço** (summary) shows people reached, actions carried out, messages sent and micro-area coverage, against the targets.
2. In the **Avaliação** (evaluation), answer **O público-alvo foi atingido?** (was the target audience reached?) and write the
   **Resultados** (results), **Conclusões** (conclusions) and **Recomendações** (recommendations).
3. **Efetividade** (effectiveness) shows the indicators associated with the campaign. **They do not prove the campaign caused the
   result.**

> The calculated figures are frozen in the evaluation record; later changes to actions do not alter it.

**Cancelar campanha** (cancel campaign): with the reason.

## 5. Common messages

| Situation | What it means | What to do |
|-----------|---------------|------------|
| Published notice without an **Editar** button | published notices cannot be edited | end it and create another |
| You cannot approve the content | you wrote it | ask someone else to review it |
| *Nenhum conteúdo revisado.* — no reviewed content | there is no approved content for the campaign | review and approve the content |
| Channel without sending | only Portal and E-mail send | it is recorded as not sent |
