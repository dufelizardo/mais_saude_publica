# Mental health

> **Version:** 1.15.0 · [Português](../pt-BR/11-saude-mental.md)

Menu: **Assistência › Saúde Mental** (Care › Mental health). It covers:

- reception (*acolhimento*) and cases under follow-up;
- the Individual Therapeutic Plan (PTS), interventions, the support network and risk;
- crises, shared care and transfer of care;
- overnight stays at 24-hour psychosocial care centres (CAPS III) and the psychosocial care network (RAPS).

> **Content restricted to the reference team.** A case only opens for those who follow up at the unit, or for the partner team
> while care is shared.

**Who uses it:** physicians, nursing, nursing coordination, psychology and social work: they receive, follow up and record
crises.

**The cards at the top:**

- the cases **Em acompanhamento** (under follow-up);
- the **Faltosos** (defaulters), who need active search;
- the cases **Sem avaliação** (without assessment);
- the cases **Sem PTS / a revisar** (no PTS / to review).

**Notices at the top:** **Crises em curso ou com notificação pendente** (ongoing crises or with a pending notification) and
**Transferências do cuidado aguardando a sua unidade** (care transfers awaiting your unit), with **Aceitar** (accept) and
**Recusar** (refuse).

## 1. Receiving (and opening the case)

1. Click **Novo acolhimento** (new reception).
2. Under **Paciente**, search by name, CPF or SUS card, and choose the **Unidade**.
3. Write the **Motivo da procura** (reason for seeking care) and, if any, the **Necessidade imediata** (immediate need) and the
   **Vulnerabilidade** (vulnerability, for example *sem renda, mora sozinho* — no income, lives alone).
4. Choose the **Risco percebido** (perceived risk).
5. Choose the **Desfecho** (outcome):
   - follow-up at the unit or shared care: the case stays with the **Equipe de referência** (reference team) and the **Técnico de
     referência** (reference professional);
   - referral: enter **Para onde** (where to, for example *CAPS AD, CRAS*);
   - crisis, or guidance without follow-up.
6. Save.

If the patient already has an open case, the reception goes into it. Otherwise, the case opens now, linked to the unit's Mental
Health programme.

> **High risk:** manage through the emergency network and record the crisis (item 6).

## 2. The case list

Search by patient, professional or team, and use the filters:

- **Todos do meu acesso** (all I can access), **Sou o técnico** (I am the professional) or **Da minha equipe** (my team);
- **Sem PTS**, **PTS a revisar**, **Risco alto** (high risk) and **Reavaliação vencida** (reassessment overdue);
- the **Unidade**.

Each row shows:

- the reference and since when;
- the last assessment and the PTS;
- the status.

Every case is marked **Sigiloso** (confidential). Its content and actions only appear for the reference team and, in shared
care, for the partner team. On each case, the quick actions are:

- **Registrar intervenção** (record intervention);
- **Nova avaliação** (new assessment);
- **Trocar referência** (change reference);
- **Encerrar** (close).

## 3. The case: assessment and PTS

The case has the sections **Resumo** (summary), **Avaliações** (assessments), PTS, **Intervenções**, **Rede de apoio** (support
network), **Rede de atenção** (care network) and **Acolhimentos** (receptions).

**Nova avaliação** (new assessment):

1. Write:
   - the **Contexto e história** (context and history) and the **Aspectos psicossociais** (psychosocial aspects);
   - the **Fatores de risco** (risk factors) and the **Fatores de proteção** (protective factors);
   - the **Necessidades identificadas** (needs identified);
   - the **Hipótese diagnóstica (CID-10)** (diagnostic hypothesis), for example *F32.1*.
2. Save.

> **Psychotherapy content does not go here:** it stays out of the shared health record, under professional confidentiality.

**The PTS** is built by the team, with the person and the family.

1. Click **Elaborar PTS** (create PTS) or **Revisar PTS** (review PTS).
2. Write the **Necessidades** (needs) and build the **Objetivos** (goals), with target, deadline and person responsible
   (**Adicionar objetivo** — add goal).
3. Build the **Intervenções previstas** (planned interventions), with type, frequency (for example, *semanal* — weekly) and the
   service involved (for example, *CRAS Centro*).
4. Enter **Revisar em** (review on). Without a date, today plus the frequency applies.
5. Tick **Pactuado com a pessoa ou a família** (agreed with the person or the family) and write the agreement note.
6. On review, write the **Síntese da revisão** (review summary, for example *adesão alcançada; novo objetivo de retorno ao
   trabalho* — adherence achieved; new goal of returning to work).

**Versões anteriores** (previous versions) are kept. A PTS with an overdue review shows as **Vencida** (overdue).

## 4. Interventions, support network and referrals

**Registrar intervenção** (record intervention):

1. Choose the type and tick **Realizada** (done) or **Não compareceu** (did not attend).
2. Enter **Quando** (when; empty means now).
3. If applicable, choose **Cumpre a intervenção prevista** (fulfils the planned intervention, from the PTS) and the sharing:
   - **Compartilhado com (matrícula)** (shared with, staff number);
   - the **Equipe matriciada** (matrix-supported team): teams with matrix support from the reference team, in either direction.
4. Write the **Resumo** (summary). For psychotherapy, record only that it happened and a summary: session content does not go to
   the shared record.

**Support network:**

1. Use **Incluir pessoa** (add person) and enter:
   - the **Nome** and the **Papel** (role): family member, carer, reference person, service or other;
   - the **Vínculo** (relationship, for example *mãe, vizinha* — mother, neighbour) and the phone.
2. To remove someone, use **Tirar da rede** (remove from network). The person stays in the case's support network history.

**Encaminhar a outro setor** (refer to another sector: CRAS and CREAS social assistance centres, school, Guardianship Council and
the other institutions in the catalogue):

1. Choose the **Instituição** and write the **Motivo** (for example, *inclusão no CadÚnico e benefício eventual* — inclusion in
   the social programmes registry and occasional benefit).
2. Enter **Enviado em** (sent on) and **Retorno esperado até** (reply expected by).
3. When the answer arrives, record the **Retorno** (reply):
   - **Concluído** (completed): the institution provided the service;
   - **Recusado** (refused): the institution did not.

The system does not access the institution: record the sending here and, later, the reply. **Without a reply on time, it becomes
a pending item for the case.** The institution catalogue is in **Administrativo › Rede intersetorial**.

**Referrals through Regulation:**

- **Nova solicitação de regulação** (new regulation request): the procedure, ICD-10 code, priority, clinical justification and
  requester. Without a staff number, it is you. Requesting needs the Regulation permission at the unit.
- **Vincular uma solicitação existente** (link an existing request): by its number.

## 5. Risk

1. On the case, click **Avaliar risco** (assess risk) or **Reavaliar** (reassess).
2. Choose the **Tipo de risco** (risk type): self-harm, harm to others, substance use, vulnerability or other.
3. Choose the **Nível** (level): no current risk, low, moderate or high.
4. Write:
   - the **Fatores identificados** (factors identified);
   - the **Medidas adotadas** (measures taken; required from moderate upwards);
   - the **Contato combinado** (agreed contact, for example *diário, semanal* — daily, weekly);
   - the **Plano de segurança** (safety plan), agreed with the person and the support network.
5. Enter **Reavaliar em** (reassess on; required for high risk).

> Each assessment is a new record: the current risk of each type is the latest one, and **no current risk** closes that type.

## 6. Crisis

1. Click **Registrar crise** (record crisis).
2. Choose the **Tipo**: self-harm, suicide attempt, harm to others, psychomotor agitation, psychotic episode, intoxication or other.
3. Enter the **Local** (place, for example *domicílio, recepção da unidade* — home, unit reception) and the **Descrição**.
4. Record the steps in the **Linha do tempo** (timeline) with **Registrar etapa** (record step): assessment, intervention, network
   activation, referral and post-crisis.
5. **Acionar o SAMU** (call SAMU, the ambulance service):
   - choose the **Central** (centre) and enter the call-back phone and the **Motivo do acionamento** (what the SAMU team needs to
     know);
   - the incident opens in Emergency 192 (module 06);
   - without a registered emergency centre, call 192 by phone and record it as a step.
6. **Encerrar a crise** (close the crisis):
   - choose the **Desfecho** (outcome): stayed at the unit, returned home, sent to emergency care, admission or other;
   - closing requires the post-crisis step.

> **Self-inflicted violence must be reported** (SINAN form, Brazil's notifiable diseases system).
>
> - Use **Notificar na Vigilância** (report in Surveillance), and the crisis becomes reported.
> - If the report was made in another system, use **Registrar notificação** (record notification) with its number.
>
> After the crisis, with a case under follow-up, reassess the risk and review the PTS.

## 7. Shared care and transfer

**Compartilhar o cuidado** (share care):

1. Choose the partner **Equipe** (team) and write the **Objetivo** (goal, for example *acompanhar a adesão à medicação na eSF* —
   follow medication adherence at the family health team).
2. While sharing is current, the partner team's members read the case content, PTS included.

**Transferir o cuidado** (transfer care):

1. Choose the **Unidade de destino** (destination unit) and write the **Resumo de contrarreferência** (counter-referral summary).
2. The destination accepts or refuses. On acceptance, this case closes with the reason *transferência* (transfer), and the case
   opens there, linked to this one. **The PTS is not copied.**

**Trocar referência** (change reference): choose the new team or professional and write the **Motivo da troca** (reason). The
previous reference stays in the case history, and the professional's HR posting does not change.

**Encerrar o caso** (close the case): choose the **Motivo da saída** (exit reason) and the **Data da saída** (exit date). Closing
ends the reference and records the exit from the Mental Health programme with the same reason. **Discharge completes it; the
other reasons close it.**

## 8. Overnight stays (CAPS III and AD III)

1. Use **Acolhimento noturno** (overnight stay) and choose the unit.
2. Set the **Vagas** (places) and the **Permanência máxima (dias)** (maximum stay in days) before the first entry.
3. **Registrar entrada** (record entry): the patient (followed up at the unit) and the expected exit.
4. In the **Acolhidos agora** (staying now) list:
   - **Prorrogar** (extend), with the justification;
   - **Saída** (exit).

The **Vencida** (overdue) and **Além do máximo** (beyond the maximum) marks warn about those past the expected exit or the limit.
**Saídas nos últimos 30 dias** (exits in the last 30 days) keeps the history.

## 9. The psychosocial care network (RAPS)

Use **Rede (RAPS)** to see the network's points by component. Each one shows:

- the units, with modalities and capabilities;
- cases and interventions in the last 90 days.

**Lacunas da rede** (network gaps) shows components without a unit.

## 10. Common messages

| Message / situation | What it means | What to do |
|---------------------|---------------|------------|
| The case opens without its content | you are not in the reference or partner team | ask the team to share care, if care requires it |
| *Notificação compulsória de violência autoprovocada pendente.* — mandatory report of self-inflicted violence pending | the self-harm crisis has not been reported yet | report through Surveillance or record the number |
| *Nenhuma central de urgência cadastrada…* — no emergency centre registered | there is no 192 centre in the system | call 192 and record it as a step |
| *O catálogo está vazio…* — the catalogue is empty | no intersectoral network institutions are registered | ask for them in Administrativo › Rede intersetorial |
| **Sem retorno no prazo** (no reply on time) | the referral passed its expected reply date | chase the institution and record the reply |
| *Encerrar pede a etapa de pós-crise.* — closing requires the post-crisis step | the crisis has no final step | record the post-crisis step |
| *Configure as vagas e a permanência máxima antes da primeira entrada.* — set places and maximum stay first | overnight stays are not configured | enter the places and the stay |
| *Seu acesso não inclui a rede de atenção psicossocial.* — your access does not include the RAPS | your profile does not see the RAPS | ask for the profile, if your role needs it |
