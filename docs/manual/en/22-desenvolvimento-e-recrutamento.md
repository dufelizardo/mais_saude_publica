# Development and recruitment

> **Version:** 1.15.0 · [Português](../pt-BR/22-desenvolvimento-e-recrutamento.md)

Menu: **Recursos Humanos › Desenvolvimento** (development) and **Recursos Humanos › Recrutamento** (recruitment).

- **Desenvolvimento:** the training catalogue, competencies and performance appraisal cycles. Each professional's participations
  and appraisals are on their profile (module 19).
- **Recrutamento:** vacancies by unit and job, and the candidates for each.

**Who uses it:** HR management (manager).

## 1. Training

**The cards at the top:**

- **Treinamentos no catálogo** (trainings in the catalogue);
- **Obrigatórios** (mandatory), required of all staff;
- **Ciclos em andamento** (cycles in progress);
- **Ciclos encerrados** (closed cycles).

**Registering a training:**

1. Enter the **Nome** (name, for example *Biossegurança e controle de infecção* — biosafety and infection control) and the
   **Carga horária (h)** (hours).
2. Enter the **Validade (meses)** (validity in months), if any. With validity, the certificate expires and requires refresher
   training.
3. Tick **Obrigatório** (mandatory) when it is required of all staff (for example, Brazilian regulation NR-32).
4. Choose the **Competências desenvolvidas** (competencies developed) and the **Material didático** (teaching material). The
   material is educational content that has passed technical review, written and reviewed in **Comunicação** (Communication).
   The training always shows the current version.
5. Save.

Search by name and use **Só obrigatórios** (mandatory only). Each professional's participation is recorded on the profile's
**Treinamentos** tab.

## 2. Competencies

Competencies are what trainings develop.

1. Use **Nova competência** (new competency): the **Código**, the **Nome** and the **Descrição**.
2. Leave **Ativa** (active) ticked. An inactive competency does not enter new trainings.

On the professional's profile, **Competências desenvolvidas** come from completed trainings. The **Vencida** (expired) mark appears
when the certificate is past its validity.

## 3. Appraisal cycles

A cycle is the period in which performance appraisals are carried out.

1. Create the cycle with the **Nome** (for example, *Avaliação de desempenho 2026* — 2026 performance appraisal), the **Início**
   (start) and the **Fim** (end).
2. Each professional's appraisal is recorded on the profile's **Avaliações** tab, within the cycle.

## 4. Recruitment

**The cards at the top:**

- **Abertas** (open) vacancies;
- **Em andamento** (in progress), with selection under way;
- **Fechadas** (closed), filled;
- **Canceladas** (cancelled), not filled.

**Opening a vacancy:**

1. Choose the **Cargo** (job) and the **Unidade**.
2. Enter the **Posições** (positions: how many people).
3. Save.

The job description (module 21) appears in the vacancy. Search by job or unit and filter by status.

**Candidates:**

1. Click the vacancy to see its candidates.
2. Use **Registrar candidato** (record candidate): the **Nome**, the **CPF** and, optionally, the **Link do currículo** (CV link).
3. Change the **Etapa** (stage) as selection moves on: registered, screening, interview, approved or rejected.
4. **Abrir** (open) shows the CV.

## 5. Common messages

| Situation | What it means | What to do |
|-----------|---------------|------------|
| *Nenhuma competência ativa no catálogo.* — no active competency in the catalogue | there is no competency to link to the training | register the competency |
| *Nenhum conteúdo revisado…* — no reviewed content | there is no reviewed educational material | write and review the content in Communication |
| **Vencido** on material or certificate | the material or certificate is past its validity | update the material or schedule refresher training |
| *Nenhum candidato registrado.* — no candidate recorded | the vacancy has no candidates | record the candidates |
